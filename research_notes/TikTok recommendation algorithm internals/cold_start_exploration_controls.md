# TikTok cold start, interest exploration, and session/user controls

Research notes compiled 2026-10-14. Dates in each bullet are the date of the feature or finding. "TikTok" below means the international app; Douyin (China) is treated separately in the last section. The US app has run on a separately retrained copy of the algorithm since 2026 (see Q3).

## Q1. How does TikTok seed a brand-new account, and how many interactions does it take to personalize?

### Takeaway
TikTok seeds new accounts from an optional interest-category picker (falling back to a generalized feed of popular videos), then personalizes primarily from watch-time, likes and follows. Audits converge on personalization that is measurable within tens to a few hundred videos (roughly 20 minutes to 1.5 hours of use), after which interest-aligned content typically occupies 50-70% of the feed (up to 93% in the WSJ's single-interest bots); TikTok's own research reportedly put the "habit-forming" threshold at 260 videos (under 35 minutes).

### Cited Findings
- Cold start (official, June 18, 2020): new users are invited to "select categories of interest, like pets or travel" to build an initial feed; for users who decline, TikTok "start[s] by offering you a generalized feed of popular videos." Signals are (1) user interactions (likes, shares, follows, comments, content created), (2) video information (captions, sounds, hashtags), (3) device/account settings (language, country, device type), with the last category weighted lower. "Watching a longer video from beginning to end" is given "greater weight than" a shared-country signal. — [TikTok Newsroom, "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou"](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- The same 2020 post says the system deliberately surfaces videos that do not "appear to be relevant to your expressed interests" so users discover new categories/creators, and that a long-press "Not Interested" lets users hide videos from specific creators or using specific sounds. — [TikTok Newsroom, 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- WSJ bot audit (published July 21, 2021): 100 fresh accounts operated by bots programmed with interests (extreme sports, dance, astrology, pets, politics, sadness/depression); bots lingered on/rewatched in-interest videos and swiped past others. Interests were identified "in under two hours"; some accounts "in less than 40 minutes." Example bot "kentucky_96": after 3 minutes and 15 videos it rewatched a sadness video; within 33 minutes (224 videos) 93% of videos served were about depression/mental health. TikTok said the remaining 7% was discovery content. Average view count of served videos fell from 6.31M (early) to 0.78M (later), i.e. the feed went "less mainstream." Guillaume Chaslot estimated "90-95%" of TikTok views come from the recommendation engine vs. >70% on YouTube. TikTok's response: the bots are not representative of real humans who have diverse interests. — [Tubefilter summary of WSJ](https://tubefilter.com/2021/07/21/tiktok-recommendation-algorithm-wall-street-journal-guillaume-chaslot/); [5Rights summary](https://5rightsfoundation.com/resource/tiktoks-secretive-algorithm-unpacked-by-the-wall-street-journal); [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2021/07/28/how-tiktoks-algorithm-works/)
- Boeker & Urman, "An Empirical Investigation of Personalization Factors on TikTok" (ACM Web Conference, April 2022; data collected June-Aug 2021): 39 completed sock-puppet scenarios, each pairing an active bot and a control bot, posts loaded in batches of ~30 (3-5 batches), 30,436 posts collected. Ranking of personalization strength by trend-line slope: follow (2.03%/batch) > video view rate i.e. watching longer (0.98%) > like (0.82%). Baseline noise between identical no-interaction accounts was already 66-70% different posts. Location mattered more than language. Authors warn findings are "only accurate for a small time window." — [ar5iv HTML of arXiv:2201.12271](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2201.12271); [arXiv abstract](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2201.12271)
- Vombatkere, Mousavi, Zannettou, Roesner, Gummadi, "TikTok and the Art of Personalization" (ACM Web Conference, May 2024): 347 users donated GDPR data (220 with 1,000+ videos; 4.9M videos viewed 9.2M times) plus 5 bots (1,000 videos each). TikTok "exploits real users' interests in between 30% and 50% of all recommended videos in the first thousand videos"; randomized baseline showed 20% exploit fraction, bot traces 31%, real users 50%+ for most of the stream. Exploit fraction rose over the first videos and "stabilized after recommendation index 100." Drivers: following creators (p=1e-14) > liking (p=1e-5) > watch percentage (p=0.03) > early skip rate (p=0.14, not significant). Exploit-labelled videos scored 0.83 on the personalization metric vs 0.08 for explore-labelled. — [arXiv HTML 2403.12410](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12410); [Tubefilter coverage](https://tubefilter.com/2024/03/21/how-tiktok-recommendation-algorithm-works-research/)
- Amnesty International, "Driven into the Darkness" (Nov 7, 2023): 30+ automated accounts posing as 13-year-olds in Kenya and the USA plus manual accounts in Kenya, Philippines, USA. After 5-6 hours, "almost 1 in 2 videos" were mental-health related and potentially harmful, roughly 10x the volume served to accounts without that interest; in manual runs, within 3-20 minutes more than half of videos related to mental-health struggles. — [EDRi summary](https://edri.org/our-work/tiktoks-for-you-feed-risks-pushing-children-and-young-people-towards-harmful-mental-health-content/); [Amnesty report page](https://www.amnesty.org/en/documents/POL40/7350/2023/en/)
- Baumann, Arora, Rahwan, Czaplicka, "Dynamics of Algorithmic Content Amplification on TikTok" (arXiv March 26, 2025; EPJ Data Science, Feb 2026): 42 bots (Gaming, Food, Gaming+Food), ~3,000 videos per bot over ~1 week, 128,148 videos (66,672 unique). Interest signalled by liking, rewatching 1-3 times and following; non-aligned videos watched 5-10%. Interest-aligned share reached 67.4% (Gaming), 52.3% (Food), 67.2% (dual). "Most onset points occur within the first 200 videos," averaging 65.7 videos (Gaming), 140 (Food), 93.2 (dual), i.e. about 1.5 hours of browsing. Hashtag diversity correlated -0.92/-0.80/-0.71 with amplification; aligned content got less popular and longer over time. — [arXiv 2503.20231](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20231v1); [EPJ Data Science 2026](https://epjds.epj.org/articles/epjdata/abs/2026/01/13688_2026_Article_629/13688_2026_Article_629.html)
- Mosnar et al. (Kempelen Institute), "Revisiting Algorithmic Audits of TikTok: Poor Reproducibility and Short-term Validity" (SIGIR, July 2025): replicated Boeker & Urman 2022 and Vombatkere 2024 (partially Mousavi 2024). Concurrent bots' hashtag similarity fell to ~10% (vs 35% in 2022), read as a "strong exploration shift." For likes/follows they saw "an exploration phase at the start, followed by strong exploitation after interacting with enough videos (around 1000)." Watch duration (esp. 400% rewatch) was the strongest factor; likes/follows were weaker than in 2022, contradicting the 2022 ranking. Changing metrics "produced completely different findings"; ~9 person-months needed to reproduce. — [arXiv 2504.18140](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.18140v1)
- Internal TikTok documents (unsealed in the Kentucky AG lawsuit; reported Oct 11, 2024): TikTok determined users become habituated after 260 videos, reachable in under 35 minutes; an employee entered "negative filter bubbles" within 20 minutes after following "painhub"/"sadnotes" accounts, and found users placed into filter bubbles after 30 minutes of continuous use. — [KPR/NPR via KAXE](https://www.kaxe.org/news/2024-10-11/tiktok-executives-know-about-apps-effect-on-teens-lawsuit-documents-allege); [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2024/10/11/g-s1-27676/tiktok-redacted-documents-in-teen-safety-lawsuit-revealed)
- Data-donation engagement study (Zannettou et al., CHI 2024; arXiv 2301.04945): 347 donors, 9.2M recommendations; daily usage rises over account lifetime while attention stays ~45%; users like more videos from followed creators than from non-followed. — [arXiv 2301.04945](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.04945v2)

### Inferences
- Audits disagree on which explicit action matters most (follow in 2022; follow/like in the 2024 real-user data; watch time in 2025), which is consistent with TikTok re-weighting signals over time; the 2025 reproduction explicitly warns that findings have short-term validity.
- The "first ~100-200 videos" onset seen by Vombatkere (stabilizes after index 100) and Baumann (avg 66-140 videos) is consistent with TikTok's own 260-video habituation figure and WSJ's 224 videos/33 minutes, so a planning number of "personalization visible within 100-250 videos / 20-90 minutes" is well supported.
- Real users' feeds are more exploitative (50%+) than single-interest bots' (31%), i.e. bots underestimate personalization depth for humans with richer signals.

### Gaps
- TikTok has never published a number for how many interactions it needs; all numbers are third-party or leaked.
- The original WSJ article is paywalled; numbers are taken from reputable secondary summaries.
- No Mozilla Foundation sock-puppet audit of TikTok's For You feed was found for 2023-2025 (Mozilla's TikTok work in that period was a data-donation tool rather than a published speed-of-personalization measurement); the 2023-2025 audits found were Amnesty (2023), Vombatkere (2024), Baumann (2025) and Mosnar (2025).

## Q2. What mechanisms does TikTok describe for interest exploration and for avoiding filter bubbles?

### Takeaway
TikTok's stated mechanisms are: interspersing videos outside expressed interests, dispersion rules (never two consecutive videos from the same creator or sound), and topic-level "repetition" limits for sensitive clusters (sadness, extreme dieting/fitness, breakups, sexually suggestive), plus content-level eligibility classification. TikTok has never disclosed an exploration traffic share.

### Cited Findings
- June 2020: TikTok says it "intersperse[s] diverse types of content" and will not show consecutive videos with the same sound or from the same creator; it also surfaces content that "doesn't appear to be relevant to your expressed interests." — [TikTok Newsroom 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- Dec 16, 2021 ("An update on our work to safeguard and diversify recommendations"): system "won't recommend two videos in a row made by the same creator or with the same sound"; TikTok is "testing ways to avoid recommending a series of similar content - such as around extreme dieting or fitness, sadness, or breakups" because content "may be fine as a single video but problematic if viewed in clusters"; also watching for feeds inadvertently narrowing to "only very limited types of content" (loneliness, weight loss). A tool to "choose words or hashtags" to exclude was announced as in development. — [TikTok Newsroom, Dec 2021](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/an-update-on-our-work-to-safeguard-and-diversify-recommendations)
- July 13, 2022: TikTok reports the repetition work shipped: "viewers now see fewer videos about these topics at a time" (dieting, fitness, sadness), noting edge cases like disordered-eating recovery content. Same post introduced Content Levels (maturity rating that keeps mature/complex-theme videos from under-18s) and announced keyword/hashtag filtering for For You and Following feeds. — [TikTok Newsroom, July 2022](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/ways-for-our-community-to-enjoy-what-they-love?lang=en-IE)
- March 16, 2023: the system "identifies repetitive themes and substitutes them with videos about other topics," specifically "sadness, extreme exercise or dieting" and "sexually suggestive" content; stated goal is to "recommend a diversity of content to enable an enriching discovery experience." — [TikTok Newsroom, March 2023](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-ie/safeguarding-recommendations-update)
- Audit evidence that exploration exists: randomized-baseline exploit fraction was ~20% and real users' feeds were 50%+ exploit, leaving roughly half of recommendations as "explore" in the first 1,000 videos (Vombatkere 2024). — [arXiv 2403.12410](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.12410)
- Audit evidence that exploration decays: Baumann 2025 found a strong negative correlation between amplification and exploration (unseen-hashtag engagement declines as alignment rises). — [arXiv 2503.20231](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20231v1)
- Audit evidence of an "exploration phase" before exploitation (~1,000 interacted videos for likes/follows) and of a 2025 feed that is more exploratory than 2022 (10% vs 35% cross-bot hashtag overlap). — [Mosnar et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.18140v1)
- The European Commission's Feb 6, 2026 preliminary DSA finding singles out the "highly personalised recommender system" together with infinite scroll, autoplay and push notifications as addictive design, and says TikTok must adapt its recommender system. — [European Commission, 6 Feb 2026](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-tiktoks-addictive-design-breach-digital-services-act)

### Inferences
- TikTok's public anti-filter-bubble story is about pattern interruption inside sensitive topic clusters and author/sound dispersion, not about a generic exploration budget; nothing published quantifies how much of the feed is exploratory.
- The 7% "discovery" share TikTok cited to the WSJ in 2021 for a single-interest bot, versus ~50% explore for real users in 2024 data and the 2025 observation of a more exploratory feed, suggests the explore share is adaptive and has increased, but these are not like-for-like measurements.

### Gaps
- No TikTok disclosure of exploration traffic share, candidate-pool size, or the exact dispersion windows was found; TikTok's Transparency Center recommendation-system page could not be fetched (JS-rendered), so wording there is not quoted directly.

## Q3. What user controls exist (as of 2026) and how do they feed back into the model?

### Takeaway
As of October 2026 the control set is: Not interested (hide creator/sound), keyword/hashtag filters (now AI-expanded "Smart Keyword Filtering", 200 keywords), Manage Topics sliders (10+ topics, frequency not exclusion), Refresh For You feed (restart as a new user, filters preserved), STEM feed (on by default in several markets), Friends/Following feeds, Restricted Mode, and in the EEA a non-personalized feed. TikTok describes these as direct inputs (hide/suppress/reset) rather than disclosing model-level mechanics.

### Cited Findings
- Not interested (2020-): long-press > Not Interested hides future videos from "a particular creator or that use a particular sound" and signals the system to "skip similar videos in the future." — [TikTok 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you); [TikTok July 2022](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/ways-for-our-community-to-enjoy-what-they-love?lang=en-IE)
- Friends tab (replacing Discover; expansion announced May 2022): a feed of videos from accounts the user is connected with, plus suggested users (contacts, mutuals). — [TechCrunch, May 9, 2022](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/09/tiktok-friends-tab-replacing-discover-tab/embed/); [SocialMediaToday](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-expands-test-of-new-friends-tab-replacing-discover/623314/)
- Keyword/hashtag filters (announced July 13, 2022): "automatically filter out videos with words or hashtags they don't want to see in their For You or Following feeds." — [TikTok Newsroom, July 2022](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/ways-for-our-community-to-enjoy-what-they-love?lang=en-IE)
- Refresh For You feed (March 16, 2023): lets users "view content on their For You feed as if they just signed up"; the system then re-learns from new interactions; it "won't override any settings you've already chosen to enable or impact accounts you've followed" (keyword filters, restricted mode, hidden creators persist). — [TikTok Newsroom, March 2023](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-ie/safeguarding-recommendations-update); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2501043)
- STEM feed: launched March 14, 2023 in the US (teens by default); expanded to Europe April 2024; from Oct 23, 2024 shown by default to all users in the US, UK and Ireland (previously default only for under-18s), toggle in Settings > Content preferences; Nov 4, 2024 newsroom adds Canada, says 25% of teens globally visit the STEM feed weekly and STEM content grew 35% since launch (TechCrunch quotes 18%); content is vetted by Common Sense Networks (appropriateness) and Poynter (reliability). — [TikTok Newsroom, Nov 2024](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/tiktok-stem-feed-now-available-to-all-users-by-default?lang=en-GB); [TechCrunch, Oct 2024](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/23/tiktok-will-now-show-its-stem-feed-to-adults-not-just-teens); [TikTok March 2023 launch](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/take-discovery-to-a-new-level-with-stem-feed?lang=en)
- Manage Topics and Smart Keyword Filtering (June 3, 2025): sliders for "over 10 popular topics" (Creative Arts, Travel, Nature, Sports, humor, current affairs, dance, food...) under Settings > Content Preferences; "won't eliminate any topics entirely, but can influence how often they're recommended." Smart Keyword Filtering uses AI to also "limit content containing similar keywords like synonyms"; keyword cap doubled from 100 to 200; keyword filters had "been used to filter over 200M words from feeds globally." Restricted Mode remains for limiting content "not comfortable for broad audiences." — [TikTok Newsroom, June 2025](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/more-ways-to-discover-new-content-and-creators-you-love); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=2846159)
- EEA non-personalized feed (Aug 28, 2023, DSA Art. 38): users can "turn off personalisation so that their For You and LIVE feeds instead recommend both locally relevant and globally 'popular' videos"; Following/Friends become chronological; search shows popular regional content; 13-17s get no personalized ads. — [TikTok Newsroom EU, Aug 2023](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-eu/compliance-digital-services-act-eu)
- US algorithm fork (2026): the TikTok USDS Joint Venture closed Jan 22, 2026 (Oracle, Silver Lake, MGX 15% each; ByteDance 19.9%); the JV must retrain "the content recommendation algorithm on U.S. user data" under license from ByteDance, with Oracle auditing. — [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/bytedance-signs-deal-for-tiktok-us-joint-venture-with-oracle-consortium); [ABC7/AP](https://abc7news.com/post/oracle-will-manage-tiktoks-algorithm-us-users-trump-administration-deal/17866333/); [Bizcommunity, Jan 27, 2026](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/tiktoks-new-owner-puts-apps-algorithm-in-the-spotlight-739022a)
- Marketing blogs claim the retrained US model fully took over the US For You feed in Q2 (April) 2026 with reach swings and more repeated videos for creators; these are SEO sources, not confirmed by TikTok or the JV. — [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/de/blog/tiktok-algorithm) (low reliability)

### Inferences
- TikTok's controls are mostly negative/suppressive (hide, filter, lower frequency) plus one reset; the only positive steering controls are Manage Topics sliders and the initial category picker, and TikTok explicitly says sliders adjust frequency rather than hard-exclude.
- "Refresh" is effectively a cold-start reset with persisted exclusion settings, which matches TikTok's 2020 cold-start description (popular/general feed then re-learn).

### Gaps
- TikTok does not disclose how Not interested / Manage Topics are implemented (e.g., as feature weights vs. post-ranking filters) or how long their effect persists.
- No official source confirms the April 2026 US-model switchover date or any behavioural differences between the US and global models.

## Q4. What time-on-app / session controls exist, who gets them by default, and what is the evidence of effect?

### Takeaway
Since March 2023 every under-18 account gets a default 60-minute daily limit (passcode to continue; under-13s need a parent passcode for +30 min), weekly screen-time recaps, sleep reminders and notification muting; since 2025 under-16s get a 10 pm "Wind Down" takeover with meditation and parents get "Time Away" scheduling. The only quantitative effectiveness evidence is TikTok's own leaked measurement: the limit cut teen usage by about 1.5 minutes/day (108.5 to 107), and the European Commission preliminarily found the tools "easy to dismiss."

### Cited Findings
- March 2, 2023: default 60-minute daily limit for under-18s, "in the coming weeks"; teens must "enter a passcode in order to continue watching, requiring them to make an active decision" (Cormac Keenan); under-13s need a parent/guardian passcode for an additional 30 minutes; teens who opt out and exceed 100 minutes/day are prompted to set a limit; weekly inbox screen-time recap; custom per-day limits, sleep reminders and notification-mute schedules for all users; threshold chosen with the Digital Wellness Lab at Boston Children's Hospital. — [Euronews, March 2, 2023](https://www.euronews.com/next/2023/03/02/tiktok-sets-60-minute-screen-time-limit-for-users-under-18-and-a-sleep-reminder-for-all)
- Pre-existing dispersion-adjacent nudges: TikTok said in Dec 2021 it was working on repetition limits as a wellbeing measure (see Q2). — [TikTok Newsroom, Dec 2021](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/an-update-on-our-work-to-safeguard-and-diversify-recommendations)
- Leaked effectiveness data (Kentucky AG suit; reported Oct 11, 2024): the 60-minute prompt reduced usage by ~1.5 minutes/day (about 108.5 to 107 minutes); a project manager wrote "Our goal is not to reduce the time spent," another that the goal was to "contribute to DAU and retention"; success was measured by "improving public trust in the TikTok platform via media coverage." Internal research tied compulsive use to "loss of analytical skills, memory formation, contextual thinking, conversational depth, empathy, and increased anxiety." — [KPR/NPR via KAXE](https://www.kaxe.org/news/2024-10-11/tiktok-executives-know-about-apps-effect-on-teens-lawsuit-documents-allege)
- March 13, 2025: "Wind Down" turns on automatically for under-16s on the app after 10 pm, interrupting the For You feed with a full-screen takeover and calming music; meditation exercises added; "Time Away" lets parents block the app on a recurring schedule via Family Pairing. — [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/03/13/tiktok-will-block-teens-on-app-after-10-pm-and-play-calming-music); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/404074/tiktok-reminds-teens-to-wind-down-at-10-pm.html)
- Nov 19, 2025: new "Time and Well-being" space replaces the screen-time page (affirmation cards, sound generator, breathing exercises, Well-being Missions incl. an 8-week Sleep Hours Mission, daily screen-time badge, weekly reflection prompt). Meditation is "automatically enabled for teens at 10pm" and "tens of millions of people have chosen to meditate"; teen accounts have "more than 50 preset safety, privacy, and security settings"; TikTok says two-thirds of teens find the time tools useful. — [TikTok Newsroom, Nov 2025](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-a-new-way-to-unwind-reset-and-recharge-on-tiktok-ca?lang=en-CA)
- Feb 6, 2026: European Commission preliminary finding that TikTok's design (infinite scroll, autoplay, push notifications, personalised recommender) breaches the DSA; existing time-management tools "are easy to dismiss and introduce limited friction," parental controls "require additional time and skills from parents"; remedies suggested include disabling infinite scroll over time, "effective screen-time breaks, including nighttime restrictions," and adapting the recommender; fines up to 6% of global turnover if confirmed. — [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-preliminarily-finds-tiktoks-addictive-design-breach-digital-services-act); [SCL summary](https://www.scl.org/european-commission-says-tiktoks-addictive-design-breaches-dsa/)

### Inferences
- The 1.5-minute effect is an internal pre/post measurement, not an RCT; still, it is the only published effect size, and it points to default limits with easy opt-out doing little.
- TikTok's 2025 features shifted from limits to "wellbeing content" (meditation, journaling), which the Commission's Feb 2026 finding treats as insufficient friction.

### Gaps
- No independent, peer-reviewed effect study of TikTok's break reminders or Wind Down was found.
- TikTok's own March 2023 newsroom post could not be fetched (URL now redirects to unrelated content); details are from Euronews reporting of that post.

## Q5. Regional/regulatory variation: EU DSA and China/Douyin

### Takeaway
The EU forces a non-personalized feed option (2023) and is now pursuing TikTok for addictive design (Feb 2026) and minors' defaults (2026); China's March 2022 algorithm provisions require an opt-out from personalization, user-tag management, and ban addiction-inducing models, and Douyin runs a mandatory 40-minute/day, 10 pm-6 am curfew youth mode for under-14s plus, since 2025, a public algorithm-disclosure site built in response to the CAC's anti-"information cocoon" campaign.

### Cited Findings
- DSA proceedings: formal proceedings opened Feb 19, 2024 covering the recommender's "rabbit hole effect," behavioural addiction, minors' default settings and age-inappropriate experiences, based partly on TikTok's Sept 2023 risk assessment. — [The Diplomat (Romania), Feb 19, 2024](https://www.thediplomat.ro/2024/02/19/european-commission-opens-formal-proceedings-against-tiktok-under-the-digital-services-act/); [Digital Policy Alert](https://digitalpolicyalert.org/event/37589-european-commission-opened-formal-investigation-into-tiktok-on-risk-assessments-and-addictive-design-features-under-digital-services-act)
- Preliminary finding on addictive design: Feb 6, 2026 (see Q4). A further preliminary finding under DSA Art. 28(1) (minors' privacy/safety) says minors' accounts must be private by default and TikTok must stop algorithmically promoting older minors' posts in the For You feed; ASIL dates it July 23, 2026 and notes no final decision yet. — [ASIL](https://asil.org/ilib/eu-commission-finds-tiktok-failed-to-protect-minors-privacy-under-digital-services-act/)
- DSA transparency: TikTok's fifth DSA transparency report (Aug 29, 2025, H1 2025) reports ~27.8M Community Guidelines removals in the EU; it focuses on moderation, not recommender metrics. — [TikTok Newsroom](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/digital-services-act-our-fifth-transparency-report-on-content-moderation-in-europe)
- China's Internet Information Service Algorithmic Recommendation Management Provisions (issued Dec 31, 2021 by CAC, MIIT, MPS, SAMR; effective March 1, 2022; 35 articles): providers "shall provide users with a choice to not target their individual characteristics, or provide users with a convenient option to switch off algorithmic recommendation services"; must let users "choose or delete user tags"; "may not set up algorithmic models ... such as by leading users to addiction or excessive consumption"; may not push to minors content that incites unsafe conduct or "harmful tendencies"; must present mainstream-value content in front pages/hot search; services with "public opinion properties" must file within 10 working days. — [DigiChina translation](https://digichina.stanford.edu/work/translation-internet-information-service-algorithmic-recommendation-management-provisions-effective-march-1-2022/); [China Law & Practice](https://www.chinalawandpractice.com/2022/05/20/provisions-for-the-administration-of-algorithmic-recommendations-in-internet-information-services)
- Implementation: by March 17, 2022 Douyin, Baidu, Taobao and WeChat offered switches to turn off recommendation algorithms. — [TechNode, March 2022](https://technode.com/2022/03/17/recommendation-algorithms-for-chinese-apps-can-now-be-turned-off)
- Douyin youth mode (announced Sept 18, 2021): real-name-registered users under 14 are placed in teenage mode automatically, limited to 40 minutes/day and locked out 10 pm-6 am; content is educational (science experiments, museums, galleries, nature); context was the June 2021 Minor Protection Law revision and gaming limits. — [CNN via WTOP, Sept 20, 2021](https://wtop.com/tech/2021/09/the-chinese-version-of-tiktok-is-limiting-kids-to-40-minutes-a-day/)
- CAC "Minor Mode" guidelines (draft Aug 2023; applies OS/app-store/app-wide): daily caps of 40 min (under 8), 1 hour (8-16), 2 hours (16-18), rest reminders, no use 10 pm-6 am, parental override. — [NBC News, Aug 2023](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/china-floats-two-hour-daily-limit-smartphone-screen-time-kids-rcna97790); [AppInChina CAC notice](https://appinchina.co/government-documents/cac-notice-on-seeking-public-comments-for-the-guidelines-for-the-development-of-minor-mode-on-the-mobile-internet-exposure-draft/)

### Inferences
- China mandates the opt-out and tag management that the EU reaches only via the DSA Art. 38 non-personalized option; neither regime has published usage rates for the opt-out.
- Douyin's 2025 disclosures name the same dispersion/exploration levers TikTok has described since 2020-2021 (scattering, interest exploration, not-interested feedback), suggesting a shared ByteDance design vocabulary, but neither side quantifies them.
