# TikTok For You feed: recommendation pipeline, signals, and objective function

Provenance labels used throughout: **[OFFICIAL]** = stated by TikTok/Douyin/ByteDance themselves (newsroom, help/transparency pages, DSA disclosures, Douyin Safety & Trust Center, ByteDance papers); **[LEAKED]** = internal documents reported by press (NYT "TikTok Algo 101", unredacted Kentucky AG complaint); **[THIRD-PARTY]** = inferred by journalists' bot studies, academic sock-puppet audits, or creator folklore. State reflected: as of 2026-10-14.

## Q1. What do the leaked and official documents say the ranking objective is?

### Takeaway
The leaked 2021 "TikTok Algo 101" document says the ranking score is a weighted sum of predicted engagement probabilities (like, comment, play, expected play-time), chosen to maximise "retention" and "time spent" in service of the "ultimate goal" of daily active users; Douyin's 2025 public disclosure states the same structure ("predicted behaviour probability x behaviour value weight = recommendation priority") but reframes the north-star as "user long-term value" across 100+ objectives. TikTok's international official pages never state a formula or a business objective, only the signal categories.

### Cited Findings
- **[LEAKED]** The NYT obtained a document titled "TikTok Algo 101", written by TikTok's engineering team in Beijing for non-technical staff; TikTok spokeswoman Hilary McQuaide confirmed its authenticity — [NYT, "How TikTok Reads Your Mind", Dec 5 2021 (syndicated at Boston.com)](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind); original at [nytimes.com](https://www.nytimes.com/2021/12/05/business/media/tiktok-algorithm.html) (paywalled; not fetched directly).
- **[LEAKED]** Simplified scoring equation in the document: **"Plike X Vlike + Pcomment X Vcomment + Eplaytime X Vplaytime + Pplay X Vplay"** — i.e. (predicted probability of like x value of a like) + (predicted probability of comment x value) + (expected play time x value) + (probability of play x value). The document adds: "The recommender system gives scores to all the videos based on this equation, and returns to users videos with the highest scores" and "the actual equation in use is much more complicated, but the logic behind is the same." — [Boston.com/NYT](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind)
- **[LEAKED]** Stated business objective: in pursuit of the company's "ultimate goal" of adding daily active users, the algorithm optimises two closely related metrics, "retention" (whether a user comes back) and "time spent." — [Boston.com/NYT](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind)
- **[LEAKED]** The document lists four main goals: "user value," "long-term user value," "creator value," and "platform value." — [Boston.com/NYT](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind)
- **[LEAKED]** The document references the "Launch Process for Douyin Recommendation Strategy" and describes itself as the "same document for TikTok and Douyin"; the NYT also found a Chinese document describing Douyin's recommender that used the exact same formula and language. — [Boston.com/NYT](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind); [Pixel Envy linklog on the NYT piece](https://pxlnv.com/linklog/tiktok-leak-nyt/)
- **[THIRD-PARTY]** Expert reactions to the formula: Guillaume Chaslot (AlgoTransparency): "This system means that watch time is key. The algorithm tries to get people addicted rather than giving them what they really want." Julian McAuley (UCSD) called it "totally reasonable, but traditional stuff." — [Boston.com/NYT](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Douyin's core logic stated publicly as a formula: "综合预测用户行为概率×行为价值权重=视频推荐优先级" (comprehensive predicted user-behaviour probability x behaviour value weight = video recommendation priority). — [Wallstreetcn, 2025-04-16](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3745312); [CLS.cn](https://cls.cn/detail/1988109)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Behaviours the models predict include 完播 (completion), 评论 (comment), 点赞 (like), 对作者长期消费 (long-run consumption of the author), 分享 (share), 跟拍 (duet/recreate). Douyin engineer 刘畅: "用户长期价值，是抖音推荐算法的'北极星指标'，也就是我们最核心的业务目标" (user long-term value is the north-star metric and core business objective). — [Xinhuanet, 2025-04-16](https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20250416/cb30d07cc64f497b938b82f551204e05/c.html)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Douyin says it has "建设和平衡了100多个像'收藏'这样的不同目标" (built and balanced 100+ objectives such as "save"), and "专门训练了预估收藏和重复观看概率的模型" (trained dedicated models predicting save and re-watch probability). Composite long-term objectives named: 收藏率 (save rate), "收藏+再访" (save + revisit), "关注+持续观看" (follow + keep watching), "打开+搜索" (open + search). — [CLS.cn](https://cls.cn/detail/1988109); [Wallstreetcn](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3745312)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok 2020]** TikTok's public description gives no formula; it says "a strong indicator of interest, such as whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end, would receive greater weight than a weak indicator, such as whether the video's viewer and creator are both in the same country." — [TikTok Newsroom, June 18 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **[LEAKED, 2024]** Unredacted portions of the Kentucky AG complaint (revealed via faulty redactions, reported by NPR/Kentucky Public Radio, Oct 2024) quote an internal TikTok study that "all it takes to get addicted is 260 videos" (watchable in under an hour); executives called time-management tools "a good talking point" and "not altogether effective"; and TikTok "retooled its algorithm to amplify users that the company deemed beautiful" after finding "a high volume" of what it called unattractive subjects in the main feed. — [NPR via KVCR, 2024-10-11](https://www.kvcrnews.org/2024-10-11/the-biggest-findings-from-uncensored-tiktok-lawsuit-documents); [NPR via GPB](https://gpb.org/news/2024/10/12/tiktok-executives-know-about-apps-effect-on-teens-lawsuit-documents-allege)

### Inferences
- The leaked TikTok formula and Douyin's 2025 public formula are structurally identical (sum over behaviours of P(behaviour) x V(behaviour)); the NYT's 2021 finding that the two platforms shared one strategy document makes it reasonable to read the Douyin 2025 disclosure as the most detailed official description of TikTok's ranking family too, with the caveat that weights, objective counts, and the "long-term value" framing may differ by market.
- "Eplaytime x Vplaytime" plus the leaked retention/time-spent goals, the WSJ's watch-time-only bot result (Q4), and the Kentucky "260 videos" habit metric all point the same way: time-on-platform and return rate, not likes, are the dominant optimisation targets. Likes/comments/shares are inputs to the score, not the goal.
- Douyin's 2025 "100+ objectives" and composite targets (save+revisit, follow+keep-watching) suggest the formula has grown far beyond the four-term 2021 version; the value weights V(.) are the unpublished part in both documents.

### Gaps
- No source publishes numerical weights (Vlike, Vcomment, Vplaytime...) for TikTok or Douyin; relative weighting is only characterised qualitatively ("strong" vs "weak" indicators).
- I could not fetch the NYT article or the CACM reprint directly (paywall/403); quotes above come from the syndicated Boston.com copy of the same column. Whether the leaked document includes explicit negative terms (dislikes/"not interested") in the equation is not confirmed in the accessible text.
- The Kentucky complaint's underlying internal documents are sealed; only the NPR-reported excerpts are available.

## Q2. How does staged exposure for new content work, and what is confirmed vs folklore?

### Takeaway
TikTok has never published tier sizes or promotion thresholds; the "200-300 initial viewers, then 1k/10k/100k pools" model is creator folklore extrapolated from observed view plateaus. What is confirmed officially is only that follower count and past performance are not direct ranking inputs, that new users get a generalised popular feed, and (from the WSJ) that fresh accounts are first served broadly popular moderator-vetted videos; Douyin's 2025 disclosure adds that scrutiny intensifies with traffic ("the higher the traffic, the more review passes and stricter standards") and that there is a 初审/复审/三审 review ladder, which is the closest any official source comes to describing tiered distribution.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok 2020]** "neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors in the recommendation system." New users pick interest categories; those who do not "receive a generalized feed of popular videos." — [TikTok Newsroom 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **[THIRD-PARTY, WSJ 2021]** Fresh bot accounts were first served "very popular videos vetted by app moderators" before the feed narrowed to niche content; initial videos averaged 6.31 million views and later niche videos averaged 0.78 million views. — [9to5mac summary of WSJ](https://9to5mac.com/2021/07/28/how-tiktoks-algorithm-works/); [Tubefilter summary of WSJ](https://www.tubefilter.com/2021/07/21/tiktok-recommendation-algorithm-wall-street-journal-guillaume-chaslot/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** "流量越高的内容经过评估的次数越多，标准也越严格" (the more traffic a piece of content gets, the more times it is evaluated and the stricter the standard); unwanted content is filtered "在不同阶段" (at different stages) via machine plus human review with a "初审、复审、三审" (first/second/third review) process. — [CLS.cn](https://cls.cn/detail/1988109); [Xinhuanet](https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20250416/cb30d07cc64f497b938b82f551204e05/c.html)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Machine review handles "宽度" (breadth — evaluating all submissions) and human review handles "深度" (depth — sensitive/professional cases). — [The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30665269)
- **[THIRD-PARTY / folklore]** Creator-marketing sources state every new video is shown to "a small test audience, usually somewhere between 200 and 350 people" and promoted only if it holds engagement (likes, comments, shares, "especially the watch-to-the-end rate"); the "views stall at ~300" phenomenon is widely reported in creator forums. — [Lilach Bullock, "Why TikTok views stop at 300"](https://www.lilachbullock.com/why-tiktok-views-stop-at-300/); [BlackHatWorld thread](https://www.blackhatworld.com/posts/20921264/)
- **[THIRD-PARTY / secondary]** A tech-explainer site claims TikTok shows a new upload "to a very small group of users, who may or may not follow the creator" and re-shows it to larger groups if reactions are favourable. This is presented as TikTok's explanation but is not in TikTok's 2020 post. — [eloutput](https://en.eloutput.com/social-media/TikTok/tiktokers-recommendation-system/)

### Inferences
- A staged/graduated exposure process is almost certainly real in some form: the Monolith architecture (Q3) trains online with minute-level parameter sync, so a new video's early engagement necessarily changes its predicted scores within minutes; Douyin's "more evaluations as traffic grows" statement describes escalating *safety* review, which implies traffic tiers exist internally. But the specific numbers (300 -> 3,000 -> 10,000 ...) and threshold metrics are unverified folklore.
- The "views stop at 300" plateau is consistent with a cold-start exploration budget, but could equally be explained by low predicted scores from the model after the first impressions; no official source distinguishes the two.

### Gaps
- No official TikTok or Douyin source gives initial-batch size, promotion thresholds, or tier counts; I found no leaked document with those numbers either.
- The Douyin Safety & Trust Center page (95152.douyin.com / trust.douyin.com/article/15358) would not render in fetch (JS-only); its cold-start section, if any, could not be read directly.

## Q3. What are the retrieval and ranking stages, and how many candidates flow through each?

### Takeaway
Douyin (2025) officially describes a recall -> filter -> ranking pipeline (with 粗排 coarse and 精排 fine ranking) built from collaborative filtering, two-tower retrieval, and Wide&Deep ranking, almost without content/user tags; ByteDance's Monolith paper (2022) describes the serving/training loop. No source discloses candidate counts per stage for TikTok or Douyin.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Pipeline: "召回、过滤、排序等环节" (recall, filtering, ranking stages). — [The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30665269); [Xinhuanet](https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20250416/cb30d07cc64f497b938b82f551204e05/c.html)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025, via press summary]** Stage descriptions attributed to the disclosure: recall (召回) "is the source and determines the ceiling of the whole recommendation"; coarse ranking (粗排) "is initial screening and generally does not use complex models"; fine ranking (精排) "is the most crucial part, with more complex features and models." — [search summary of CLS/95152 coverage](https://cls.cn/detail/1988109) (verbatim not confirmed in the fetched body)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Models named: 协同过滤 (collaborative filtering), 双塔召回 (two-tower retrieval — user and content features converted to numerical "fingerprints" and compared), Wide&Deep (single-layer "wide" for memorisation + multi-layer "deep" for generalisation). — [TechNode, 2025-04-01](https://technode.com/2025/04/01/bytedances-douyin-reveals-algorithm-amid-government-push-to-tackle-online-platform-issues/); [The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30665269)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** "抖音算法已几乎不依赖对内容和用户打标签" (the algorithm almost no longer relies on tagging content or users); it operates "在完全'不理解内容'的情况下" (without understanding content) by directly predicting "每一个用户对每一个内容的目标行为，比如点赞、关注、分享、评论的概率" (each user's probability of target behaviours on each item) and selecting the highest-probability items. — [CLS.cn](https://cls.cn/detail/1988109); [Xinhuanet](https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20250416/cb30d07cc64f497b938b82f551204e05/c.html)
- **[OFFICIAL, ByteDance 2022]** Monolith (ByteDance/Fudan, RecSys 2022 ORSUM workshop) describes the production loop: "user actions log and features [are] streamed into the online joiner," which "concatenates features with labels from user actions and produces training examples"; parameters sync from training to serving parameter servers at minute-level intervals for sparse embeddings and day-level for dense parameters; collisionless cuckoo-hash embedding tables with frequency filtering and expiry; terabyte-scale models with ~1000 embedding tables; ~400 MB/min incremental sparse sync; online training gave 14-18% AUC gains vs batch in 7-day live A/B tests. Deployed in "BytePlus Recommend." — [arXiv 2209.07663](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07663)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok 2020]** Signals grouped as user interactions, video information ("captions, sounds, and hashtags"), and device/account settings; TikTok does not describe stages. — [TikTok Newsroom 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **[THIRD-PARTY]** Guillaume Chaslot estimated that on TikTok "90-95% of the content that is seen ... comes from the recommendation engine," versus 70%+ on YouTube. — [Tubefilter/WSJ](https://www.tubefilter.com/2021/07/21/tiktok-recommendation-algorithm-wall-street-journal-guillaume-chaslot/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Oracle/Project Texas]** Oracle began inspecting portions of TikTok's source code in the Dedicated Transparency Center (live Jan 2023) and has "regular vetting and validation" of the AI tools for moderation and For You discovery, to "ensure that outcomes are in line with expectations and that the models have not been manipulated in any way"; Oracle cannot change the algorithms. — [Axios scoop, 2022-08-16](https://www.axios.com/2022/08/16/oracle-auditing-tiktok-algorithms); [Inverse/Input](https://www.inverse.com/input/tech/oracle-reviewing-tiktok-algorithms-project-texas-china); [Bloomberg via BNN](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/tiktok-will-soon-grant-oracle-full-access-to-code-algorithm-1.1923502)
- **[OFFICIAL/press, 2025 US deal]** Under the Sept 2025 US joint-venture deal, ByteDance licenses a copy of the algorithm to the US buyers and Oracle "recreates and retrains the algorithm 'from the ground up'" on US data; a White House official (via FT) said Oracle's remit runs "from source code review, to algorithm retraining, to application development and deployment." — [MediaPost, 2025-09-22](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/409287/oracle-to-recreate-tiktoks-algorithm-under-new-de.html)

### Inferences
- The TikTok (international) stack and Douyin's are the same ByteDance lineage (NYT: one shared strategy document; Monolith is ByteDance-wide infrastructure), so the Douyin-described recall/coarse/fine structure is the best available map of TikTok's pipeline, though the Oracle retraining under the 2025 US deal may cause the US model to diverge over time.
- "Content understanding and tagging" in the sense of a taxonomy is, by Douyin's own account, largely gone from ranking; video metadata (captions, sounds, hashtags) still enter as features, and content classification clearly still exists for *safety* filtering (Douyin's machine+human review; TikTok's "ineligible for recommendation" notices under the DSA).
- The Oracle review has not produced any public description of the algorithm; its reporting is about *who may inspect*, not *what was found*.

### Gaps
- No disclosed candidate counts per stage for either platform. (A ByteDance 2025 paper, "RankMixer: Scaling Up Ranking Models in Industrial Recommenders," [arXiv 2507.15551](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15551), surfaced in search as Douyin-deployed ranking work but was not fetched or verified in this pass.)
- I found no public Oracle report on TikTok's code; nothing has been released from the Project Texas review describing ranking logic.

## Q4. Which signals are used, how are negative signals and diversity applied, and what is the provenance of each claim?

### Takeaway
TikTok officially lists watch-to-end/completion, likes, shares, comments, follows, content created, captions/sounds/hashtags, language/country/device (low weight), and "Not interested"; the WSJ bot study and Boeker & Urman's audit independently show dwell/watch time and follows are the strongest inputs. Diversity is applied as explicit de-duplication rules (no two consecutive videos with the same sound or creator), penalty terms for same-author/same-tag repetition, and "forced recommendation"/dispersion, confirmed by both the leaked document and official posts.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok 2020]** Signals: user interactions ("videos you like or share, accounts you follow, comments you post, and content you create"), video information ("captions, sounds, and hashtags"), and device/account settings ("language preference, country setting, and device type"), the last receiving lower weight since users don't actively express them as preferences. Finishing a longer video is a "strong indicator"; same-country is a "weak indicator." — [TikTok Newsroom 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok 2020]** Negative signal: long-press -> "Not Interested"; users can hide content from a creator or sound. — [TikTok Newsroom 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok 2020]** Diversity/dedup rules: the system "generally won't show two videos in a row made with the same sound or by the same creator," won't recommend "duplicated content, content you've already seen before, or any content that's considered spam," and deliberately surfaces videos outside expressed interests to provide "a diversity of videos." — [TikTok Newsroom 2020](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you)
- **[OFFICIAL, TikTok DSA 2023]** "Why this video" explanations cite: content watched, liked, shared, comments posted, searches, accounts followed, suggested accounts, or content popular/recently posted in the region. The non-personalised EU feed shows popular content from the user's "region" and "preferred language." Users are notified when content is "ineligible for recommendation." — [TechCrunch, 2023-08-04](https://techcrunch.com/2023/08/04/tiktok-algorithm-dsa/); [ISD 2025](https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/towards-transparent-recommender-systems-lessons-from-tiktok-research-ahead-of-the-2025-german-federal-election/)
- **[LEAKED]** The Algo 101 document discusses countering boredom by adding values such as "same_author_seen" and "same_tag_today" to the equation, plus "forced recommendation in users' for u feed and dispersion." — [Boston.com/NYT](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind)
- **[LEAKED]** A person who read the document was concerned the app pushes users toward "sad" content that could induce self-harm. — [Boston.com/NYT](https://www.boston.com/news/commentary/2021/12/06/how-tiktok-reads-your-mind)
- **[THIRD-PARTY, WSJ 2021]** 100 bot accounts with assigned interests; bots only lingered/rewatched on matching videos and never liked/followed. Finding: "How long you linger over a piece of content" alone was enough; some accounts had interests identified in under 40 minutes, one in under 3 minutes; the depression-persona bot "kentucky_96" reached a feed that was 93% depression/sadness content after 36 minutes / 224 videos. TikTok said the remaining 7% was discovery diversity and that bot behaviour "isn't representative of real users." — [Tubefilter/WSJ](https://www.tubefilter.com/2021/07/21/tiktok-recommendation-algorithm-wall-street-journal-guillaume-chaslot/); [9to5mac/WSJ](https://9to5mac.com/2021/07/28/how-tiktoks-algorithm-works/); original: [WSJ, July 21 2021](https://www.wsj.com/articles/tiktok-algorithm-video-investigation-11626877477)
- **[THIRD-PARTY, academic]** Boeker & Urman (WWW '22): sock-puppet audit, ~20 runs per scenario, 39 completed scenarios (June-Aug 2021), 30,436 posts. All tested factors (language, location, follow, like, video view rate) influenced recommendations; ordering: "(1) following specific content creators, (2) watching certain videos for a longer period of time, and finally (3) liking specific posts." Divergence slopes: follow 2.03%, view-rate persona 0.98%, like 0.82%, control 0.42%. Authors warn findings "may be only accurate for a small time window." — [arXiv 2201.12271](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12271)
- **[THIRD-PARTY, academic/NGO]** ISD (2025) and cited studies found "Why this video" explanations can be inaccurate (accounts that never commented told "You commented on similar videos"); platforms "prioritised transparency without adequately addressing controllability"; in a German-election audit, AfD fan pages were "disproportionately represented among the first political videos shown." — [ISD](https://www.isdglobal.org/digital-dispatch/towards-transparent-recommender-systems-lessons-from-tiktok-research-ahead-of-the-2025-german-federal-election/)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Diversity/exploration measures: 多样性打散 (diversity scattering/re-ranking), 多兴趣召回 (multi-interest recall), 扶持小众兴趣 (supporting niche interests), random recommendations, social-relationship discovery, hot-topic sections with multiple viewpoints, and "exploration-class indicators"; "算法还会刻意进行多样化推荐，以免同质化内容让用户审美疲劳." Negative feedback: long-press "不感兴趣", keyword blocking, content-preference management. — [Xinhuanet](https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20250416/cb30d07cc64f497b938b82f551204e05/c.html); [Huxiu](https://m.huxiu.com/article/4248694.html); [Wallstreetcn](https://wallstreetcn.com/articles/3745312)

### Inferences
- Across all three evidence classes, implicit dwell/completion dominates explicit actions: the leaked formula has two play-time terms out of four; the WSJ needed only linger time; Boeker & Urman rank view-rate above likes (but below follows, which are stronger per-account signals because they create a direct creator->user edge).
- "Dispersion" in the leaked document and Douyin's "打散" are the same re-ranking concept: a post-scoring diversification step that penalises consecutive same-author/same-topic items, i.e. diversity is enforced as a constraint/penalty layer rather than inside the objective.
- Negative signals (Not interested, skips) are confirmed as inputs but no source quantifies their weight relative to positives; the WSJ's rapid rabbit-holing with no explicit negatives shows skipping alone is a weaker counterweight than lingering is a positive.

### Gaps
- No official or leaked source gives weights for negative feedback, rewatch, or dwell vs completion separately.
- TikTok's current Transparency Center recommender page (tiktok.com/transparency/en-us/recommendation-system/) and Help Center page rendered only a title in fetch; wording for 2024-2026 (e.g., STEM feed, keyword filters, "refresh" feed) was not captured verbatim.

## Q5. What did Douyin's 2025 disclosure add that was new, and how does it differ from TikTok's international description?

### Takeaway
Douyin's Safety & Trust Center (launched 2025-03-30, with an "open day" on 2025-04-15/16) is the first *official* ByteDance-family statement of the ranking formula, the recall/coarse/fine pipeline, the model families, the "no tags" claim, the 100+-objective multi-task framing with "user long-term value" as north star, and the explicit anti-filter-bubble mechanisms; TikTok's international pages remain at the 2020 level of "signal categories plus user controls," with DSA additions limited to explanations and a non-personalised feed.

### Cited Findings
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** Douyin president 韩尚佑 announced the Safety and Trust Center website on March 30, 2025, publishing algorithm principles, community norms, governance, and user service mechanisms; the site was in trial operation soliciting feedback. — [TechNode](https://technode.com/2025/04/01/bytedances-douyin-reveals-algorithm-amid-government-push-to-tackle-online-platform-issues/); [CLS.cn](https://cls.cn/detail/1988109)
- **[OFFICIAL, Douyin 2025]** An "安全与信任中心开放日" (open day) in Beijing on April 15, 2025 presented the algorithm; coverage dated April 16. — [Xinhuanet](https://www.xinhuanet.com/tech/20250416/cb30d07cc64f497b938b82f551204e05/c.html); [The Paper](https://www.thepaper.cn/newsDetail_forward_30665269)
- **[CONTEXT]** The disclosure followed a three-month campaign launched late Nov 2024 by the Cyberspace Administration and other agencies targeting algorithmic problems such as filter bubbles; in January 2025 Douyin announced ten transparency measures, and on Jan 8 Douyin VP 李亮 had rejected the idea that its algorithms create filter bubbles. — [TechNode](https://technode.com/2025/04/01/bytedances-douyin-reveals-algorithm-amid-government-push-to-tackle-online-platform-issues/)
